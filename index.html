Tips for you

    The U.S. EPA guess assumes about at least 30% of household water is used outdoors and that smart irrigation trims that by about 30%. The irrigation calculator uses your own numbers and UF/IFAS conversion factors. Recommendations and tips come from simple rules. The optional AI advisor uses Google’s Gemini model. Your entries are saved in this browser, and also to your account when you are signed in. Watering rules shown are Broward County’s year-round schedule, and temporary drought orders can override them, so check sfwmd.gov. Built for the Congressional App Challenge 2026.
